# Issue 4: Card content, rules, and lifecycle

## Card content

Cards are generated from curated raid-specific and raid-generic pools. A raid-specific card randomly includes 2–8 unique squares from the selected raid and fills remaining cells from the generic pool. Raid Generic uses only generic content. The center cell is always Free Space. Catalog size is being expanded in reviewable batches; earlier 500+ claims were not verified.

## Rules

- Players can mark and unmark any non-center cell; marks are local and self-reported.
- The free center counts automatically.
- Horizontal and vertical lines count; diagonals do not.
- A card announces bingo once after a completed line, using `/yell` and a random quote, then `/laugh`.
- Optional automark matches only supported combat-log or chat observations against the active card. Ambiguous social moments remain manual.

## Lifecycle

Select a deck from the dropdown or slash command, mark cells during the raid, and use `/rb new` or `/rb reset` to start over. The current card and marks persist locally.

## Authoring

Keep square text concise enough for small cells, avoid duplicate prompts, and review encounter-specific mechanics for accuracy. The in-client smoke checklist remains the final behavior check.
